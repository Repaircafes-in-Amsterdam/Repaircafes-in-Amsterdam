There are now around 40 Repair Cafés active in Amsterdam. A wonderful network of volunteers dedicated to repairing, reusing and creating a more sustainable city.

To support and further strengthen this network, we want to set up an umbrella foundation.

To this end, we are looking for committed people who want to help build the future of Repair Cafés in Amsterdam.

Are you good at bringing people together, do you have a head for finance, or are you a good organizer? If so, you can make a valuable contribution. You don't need to be a repairer or already involved as a volunteer at a Repair Café.

We want to form a small, practical and effective board that supports, connects and represents the network. The role involves a manageable time commitment and plenty of scope for taking the initiative.

<Header>Who are we looking for?</Header>

- **Chair:** someone with board experience who brings people together, establishes and maintains contacts, and has an affinity for sustainability and social initiatives.
- **Treasurer:** someone with financial acumen, preferably with experience of charities, grants or funds.
- **Secretary:** someone who brings structure, follows up on agreements and enjoys organizing.

<Header>Time commitment for 2027</Header>

- 4 board meetings per year.
- One annual networking event.
- An average of 2-3 hours per month.

<Header>What is not part of your role</Header>

- You will not have any day-to-day operational responsibilities.
- You will not be required to organize Repair Café events yourself.
